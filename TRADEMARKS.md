# Trademarks

Apache-2.0 §6 grants no rights in trade names, trademarks or product names. "Handout",
its wordmark and any logo are therefore not part of the licence grant.

Fine:

- describing your fork factually — "a fork of Handout", "based on Handout";
- keeping the name inside an unmodified copy of the project.

Not fine:

- naming a fork, a product or a service "Handout" or something confusable with it;
- using the name or logo in a way that looks like this project, or like an endorsement by
  it.

A fork that diverges gets its own name. Ask if you are unsure.

There is no registered mark to point at — this is an unregistered mark, and neither ™ nor
® belongs anywhere near it.
